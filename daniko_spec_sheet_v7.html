SCHEDULE A — WINDOW WATERPROOFING STANDARD
Requirement Detail
Scope Applies to any window located inside a tub or shower surround. This is the mandatory waterproofing method for that condition — not optional, not a "best effort" item.
Frame Build-Out Build out the window frame using Schluter KERDI-BOARD (see Wall Waterproofing spec, Bathroom section) so that sealant bonds directly to the window's vinyl frame — not to backer board. Backer board is not an acceptable bonding surface at the window perimeter.
Bottom Plate Bottom plate goes in first and must be pitched toward the shower/tub to shed water away from the frame — no flat or reverse-pitched bottom plates.
Screw Holes Every screw hole in the frame and build-out must be sealed. No exposed/unsealed fasteners anywhere in the wet zone.
Edge Bonding Bottom, side, and top edges of the window frame must be fully bonded — full contact seal, no gaps, no partial runs of sealant.
⚠ Pass/Fail: This detail is inspected at the progress-payment walkthrough. Any window inside a tub/shower surround that does not meet the above (frame build-out, pitched bottom plate, sealed screw holes, fully bonded edges) fails inspection and progress payment will not be released until corrected.
